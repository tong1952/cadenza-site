Questions, answered

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Cadenza runs entirely on your computer — the AI separation, the pitch shift and the video rendering all happen locally. Nothing is sent over the internet except fetching a video when you paste a link.

Which formats can I use?

Common video formats like MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM and M4V. The result is saved as a karaoke video plus an MP3 instrumental in your Downloads folder.

Why do I sometimes hear a faint vocal residue?

When a sung note hits the exact same pitch as an instrument at the same moment, even the best AI cannot fully separate them — you may hear a ghost of the voice there. It is rare, and the karaoke preview lets you judge the result before you render.

Does pitch shifting change the speed?

No. The tempo stays exactly the same — only the key moves, so your karaoke video stays in sync with the original performance.

What does the Pro version add?

Pro gives you unlimited tracks (the free version includes 3), the batch queue for whole playlists, the highest-quality separation model, and priority support — with a one-time payment, no subscription.
